People who are good at what they do stop at a different point.

Isn't this enough? No. Right when that stopping point starts to feel natural, the people who are good keep going, one step, then two. Every result, every product ends up as polished as the attention paid to its details. I know that, and yet I only really notice it once I have reached the end.

Detail turned out to be persistence of thought. Everyone I wanted to learn from stopped thinking at a different point. But you never see this kind of problem when you only work by yourself, so I want to keep running into the bar above me, reading it, learning from it. How? For a start, I will walk my thinking just two steps further.

1. If someone said this was not good, where would they point?
2. Compare my result against a higher-bar result and write the difference in one line.

This is a resolution I am writing after a piece of writing sent me into a brief spiral of self-doubt and I pulled myself back out. I want to flick the forehead of the me who built something half-baked and thought it was good enough. Not just in studying technology but in everything I face, I want to spend the effort this takes and close the gap at the stopping point to within a single step. I hope that every three, six, nine months, and a year, I can feel the bar rising a little. Always Awake.
